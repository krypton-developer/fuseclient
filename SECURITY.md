# Staying safe

## The only official places

- Download: **https://dashboard.fuseclient.com**
- Website: **https://fuseclient.com**
- Discord: **https://discord.gg/um7saN5vjh**
- This GitHub repo (no downloads here)

Anything else calling itself Fuse, including "cracked" or "free premium" versions, is fake and may steal your account.

## Rules that keep you safe

- Staff **never DM you first**.
- Never share your licence key or your Fuse jar. Sharing can get your licence revoked.
- Link your key to Discord. Don't log in with only the key.

## Found a security problem?

Don't post it in a public issue. Message staff in the Discord and say it's a security report.

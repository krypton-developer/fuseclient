# FAQ

### Is Fuse really free?
Yes. Log in with Discord on https://dashboard.fuseclient.com and download. Your key is made for you and linked to your Discord account, so there's nothing to redeem.

### What does "Connect Discord" see?
Only your Discord name and profile picture. Not your messages, servers or email.

### How do I open the menu?
Press **Right Shift** in game.

### How do I use a module?
Click it to turn it on. Open its drawer to change settings. Any module can get a keybind, and modules that do something on a key press have an **Activator Key** setting.

### What does the red or yellow sign next to a module mean?
That module is easy for servers to notice. Be careful with it. No module is ever guaranteed safe.

### How do I share my config?
Open the config page and make a code. The short 4 character code works for 5 minutes. There are also public configs anyone can browse, and cloud configs that follow you to any PC.

### I got a new PC
Your licence locks to the first PC you play on. On the dashboard go to **Tools > Reset HWID**. You get 2 resets per month.

### A module does nothing
Check it's turned on and look at its settings. If it still doesn't work, open a **Bug report** issue here or ask in the Discord.
